## Brand & Style

SmartCart ML pairs algorithmic precision with the tactile luxury of modern editorial commerce. The aesthetic blends contemporary **Corporate/Modern** clarity with subtle **Glassmorphic** and **Tactile** accents: crisp architectural layouts, luminous surfaces, and hyper-legible metric layers.

The brand persona is analytical, trustworthy, discerning, and effortless. Interfaces must feel fast, light, and transparent, avoiding algorithmic black-box vibes in favor of clear, explainable, and confidence-building product recommendations.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intelligent financial baseline with high-contrast text and purposeful state accents:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5`)**: Electric indigo for system primaries, predictive highlights, active selection states, and principal calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: Resonant emerald representing budget health, positive delta/savings tags, and ML match scores exceeding target thresholds.
- **Tertiary (`#F43F5E`)**: Refined crimson/rose deployed strictly for over-budget friction, constraints, and negative financial warnings.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Deep obsidian/navy for foundational typography and high-density interface anchors.
- **Surfaces**: Layered off-whites (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) with crisp stark-white container centers (`#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates structural voice from machine-driven metrics:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Contemporary geometry with friendly human touches, conveying premium e-commerce curation.
- **Body (Inter)**: Industry standard for neutral legibility across product metadata, descriptions, and user inputs.
- **Data & System Tags (Space Grotesk)**: Monospaced-leaning geometric clarity reserved exclusively for financial figures, machine-learning confidence tags, and budget telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid structured with strict content constraints:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns, max container width of 1440px, `margin-lg` (3rem) edge boundaries, and `gutter-lg` (2rem) separation. Sidebar panels for real-time budget telemetry occupy 4 columns, product grids occupy 8 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). The budget tracker transitions to a sticky top/bottom drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Product cards scale to single or dual-column feeds with fluid card widths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of **Tonal Layers**, **Micro-Borders**, and **Ambient Shadow Tinting**:

- **Card Surfaces**: Pure `#FFFFFF` on top of `#F8FAFC` background canvas, bounded by a 1px micro-border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- **Soft Ambient Elevation (Cards)**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Hover State**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` paired with border tint shifts to `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.2)`.
- **Budget Control HUD & Floating Elements**: Translucent frosted containers with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, and subtle drop shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface leverages `rounded-2xl` for macro-containers and standard geometric scales for sub-elements:

- **Cards & Modals**: 1rem (16px) standard; primary product cards and floating panels apply 1.5rem (24px) for a soft, curated finish.
- **Buttons, Segmented Tabs, and Input Fields**: 0.5rem (8px) to maintain purposeful interactive density.
- **ML Explainer Badges & Tags**: Full pill styling (9999px) to distinctly isolate automated metadata from interactive rectangular inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#4F46E5` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, 0.5rem border-radius, font `Inter` weight 600. Subtle shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary/Ghost**: Micro-border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive/Warning**: `#F43F5E` tinted backgrounds (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.08)`) with matching stroke and solid fill on critical triggers.

### ML Recommendation & Budget Badges
- **Budget-Compliant Tag**: Pill shape, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.10)`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)`. Preceded by an icon or metric dot.
- **Over-Budget Tag**: Pill shape, background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.08)`, text `#BE123C`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.20)`.
- **ML Confidence Score**: Space Grotesk typography, micro pill with indigo wash (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`), text `#4338CA`, displaying match percentages (e.g., `98% Match`).

### Interactive Budget Meters
- Track: `#E2E8F0` height 8px with full radius.
- Fill: Gradient spanning `#4F46E5` to `#10B981` during compliant levels. Transitions dynamically to `#F43F5E` when the projected cart total breaches user-defined thresholds.
- Dynamic Tooltip: Displays remaining balance in real time using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

### Product Cards
- Container: White background, 24px border radius, 1px border `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- Image Container: Subtle off-white base (`#F8FAFC`) with 16px radius inset.
- Metadata Layout: Upper row displays ML reason ("Matches: Past tech purchases"), followed by product title in `headline-sm`, bottom anchor showing price, delta savings, and instantaneous "Add to Budget" CTA.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs have a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), 8px border radius, resting height 44px, and focus state utilizing an indigo highlight ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
